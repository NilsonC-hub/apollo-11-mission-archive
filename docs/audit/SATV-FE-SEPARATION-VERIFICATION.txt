### Section 12: Separation (PDF page 183, printed page 12-1)

12.1 SUMMARY:
  "S-IC/S-II first plane separation was satisfactory. Related data indicate
  that the S-IC retromotors performed as expected. Similarly, S-II second
  plane separation and S-II/S-IVB separation were nominal."

12.2 S-IC/S-II SEPARATION EVALUATION:
  "S-IC/S-II separation and associated sequencing were accomplished as
  planned. Dynamic conditions at separation were within staging limits.
  Rate gyros and accelerometers located on the Instrument Unit (IU)
  showed no disturbances, indicating a clean severance of the stages."

12.3 S-II/S-IVB SEPARATION EVALUATION:
  "The S-II retromotors and the S-IVB ullage motors performed
  satisfactorily and provided a nominal S-II/S-IVB separation. Dynamic
  conditions at separation were within staging limits with separation
  conditions similar to those observed on previous flights."
