Apollo 11 Transcripts

 

The raw transcripts linked from this page are presented in their original form, as provided by NASA to the press during the mission. They have not been corrected; no copyright is asserted. They are presented, in part, for historical completeness. The corrected transcript which forms the foundation of the Apollo 11 Lunar Surface Journal, starting with the chapter titled "The First Lunar Landing", is covered by copyright.
