Apollo 11 Flight Plan

 :

PDF versions of the raw scans were supplied by Glen Swanson, History Office, NASA Johnson Spaceflight Center, and Steve Garber, History Office, NASA Headquarters, February 2001.  Shelly Kelly, University of Houston - Clear Lake, provided scans of pages missing from the Final Version -  3.3 to 3.8, inclusive - after we were alerted in April 2010 by Alessandro Gentilini.

Adobe Acrobat Reader is required to read PDF files and can be downloaded at no cost.

 Three version are linked below:

  • 15 April 1969 Preliminary Version
